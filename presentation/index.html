CRITICAL INTEGRITY VULNERABILITIES IN MODERN DIGITAL EVIDENCE LIFECYCLES
⚠️ 1. Silent Evidence Tampering & Bit-Rot
Digital exhibits stored on generic drives or cloud buckets can be altered, replaced, or scrubbed of metadata without leaving any tamper trace before trial.
KEY IMPACT: Over 30% of cyber cases face integrity challenges in court.
📄 2. Broken Paper-Based Chain of Custody
Physical logbooks and scattered spreadsheets lead to unrecorded custodian handoffs, missing origin timestamps, and untraceable evidence transfers.
KEY IMPACT: Custody gaps represent the #1 reason for evidence dismissal.
⚖️ 3. High Inadmissibility in Courtrooms
Under Indian Evidence Act Sec 65B and US FRE 902(14), electronic evidence must prove self-authenticating hash provenance. Lack of certified hashes ruins prosecutions.
KEY IMPACT: Section 65B certification requires verifiable mathematical proof.
🔒 4. Zero Public & Judicial Transparency
Judges, defense attorneys, and oversight bodies have no method to verify that court exhibits match original police seizures without exposing confidential raw data.
KEY IMPACT: Zero-knowledge verification is completely absent in legacy workflows.
🚨 THE RESULT: Flawed chain-of-custody documentation results in wrongful acquittals, compromised evidence, and lost judicial trust.
EVICHAIN: SOVEREIGN DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT & CRYPTOGRAPHIC LEDGER
[01] Server-Side SHA-256 Fingerprinting
Instantaneous cryptographic checksum generation directly upon file ingestion stream. The 64-character hex hash is mathematically immutable and verified against storage integrity.
ARCHITECTURE: Node.js crypto streams · Zero plaintext disk exposure
[02] Immutable Custody Ledger & RBAC
Every single action (View, Transfer, Download, Annotation) is written to an append-only audit trail capturing actor ID, role, exact timestamp, IP address, and cryptographic signature.
ARCHITECTURE: PostgreSQL append-only · 4-tier Role Scoping (Admin/Inv/Aud/Cust)
[03] Forensic Readiness Engine (0–100)
Continuous automated heuristic scoring evaluating physical storage, hash matches, custody continuity, and access velocities. Non-negotiable Critical Override locks compromised cases at At-Risk.
ARCHITECTURE: Deterministic scoring · Critical Override Rule Enforcement
[04] Zero-Knowledge Public Verification
Open public verification portal allows judicial officers, defense counsel, and the public to confirm exhibit authenticity via SHA-256 hash or file drop without accessing private payloads.
ARCHITECTURE: Zero-knowledge verification · Section 65B PDF Certificates with QR
✅ COMPLETE DELIVERABLE: Court-admissible forensic PDF certificates generated in 1-click with verification QR codes.

FOUNDATIONAL FORENSICS STANDARDS, STATUTORY ACTS & TECHNICAL RESEARCH
📚 ISO / IEC 27037:2012 : Guidelines for Digital Evidence Handling
Ensures seized digital artifacts maintain cryptographic integrity and follow an uninterrupted chain of custody without alteration. [Core foundation of EviChain's custody tracking].
📚 NIST SP 800-86 : Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response
Authoritative federal guide establishing best practices for digital media examination, SHA-256 cryptographic verification, and detailed audit log generation. [Model for EviChain's integrity engine].
📚 Indian Evidence Act — Section 65B : Admissibility of Electronic Records
Statutory legal mandate requiring electronic evidence to be certified with verifiable provenance to be admissible in Indian courts (Anvar v. Basheer precedent). [Basis for 1-click Section 65B PDF export].
📚 US FRE Rule 902(14) : Self-Authenticating Electronic Data via Digital Fingerprints
Authorizes digital records to be self-authenticating in federal courts if certified by a cryptographic hash match. [Direct validation for EviChain's public verification portal].
📚 RFC 6962 & Merkle Hash Trees : Cryptographic Transparency Logs
Demonstrates how hash chaining delivers mathematically verifiable immutability without the latency and cost of public blockchains. [Underpins EviChain's zero-gas-fee architecture].
